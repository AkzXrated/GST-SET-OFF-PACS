    Rule 38 (Section 17(4) CGST Act) Banking ITC 50% Discard Inspector
    Why Bank HO SA 0200 Contains 100% Eligible Credit

    Under Section 17(4) of the CGST Act, 2017, banking companies & co-operative banks are mandated to claim only 50% of eligible input tax credit every month.

    Vadakkumbad SCB SOP: At purchase entry, invoices are expensed 50% directly into expenditure ledgers, and only the remaining 50% eligible tax is debited into SA 0200. Therefore, SA 0200 in PacScore represents 100% net claimable credit, requiring zero post-return reversal at set-off time.
